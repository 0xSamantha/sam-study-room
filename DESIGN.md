# The Reading Room
A quiet digital reading desk, with no audio, video, or ornamental activity.
Typography: Georgia for literary headings and the large tabular timer; system sans for controls and small labels. Palette: ivory paper #f5f2e9, ink #293e34, muted forest #536d58, pale sage #e4e9dd, warm gray #817f73. Layout: a narrow library masthead, generous central timer card, initials on bookplate-like member tiles, and a compact optional correspondence panel. Mobile stacks into one column with generous touch targets. Imagery: CSS linework and a small open-book mark; no stock photos. Motion: subtle state transitions only, honoring reduced motion. Diagnostics and usage belong in an owner-only drawer. Timer and presence are deliberate interaction feedback.

## Trader Samz’ Study Room — table and schedule
Preserve the warm paper, ink, green accent and Georgia typography. Replace bookplate tiles with a literal round wooden reading table, subtle concentric grain and twelve numbered seats on its circumference. Occupied seats glow as steady LED dots, with readable names and owner labels so color is never the only signal. The owner LED is fixed pink (#ff5ca8); guests choose from blue, green, amber, violet, cyan and orange. Seats are buttons with clear selected/focus states; no flashing or pulsing. At phone widths keep the circle intact with compact labels and a text seat list for clarity. The schedule is a paper card beside the shared clock, showing dated sessions in each viewer’s local time with an explicit timezone. Owner-only scheduling uses labeled date/time inputs and a publish/remove workflow. No photography or generated assets; CSS/SVG supports crisp, accessible rendering without adding network overhead. Respect reduced motion and avoid continuous animation.

## Optional video queue and adjustable clock
Remove the decorative table and fixed-duration footer captions. Keep focus/break labels tied to actual owner-configured durations. Use a compact, owner-only duration form with a clear notice that saving resets and pauses the active clock. Add a full-width paper video card below the table: shared ordered queue, labeled YouTube URL/title inputs, and a responsive embedded player that loads only on an explicit Watch action. Playback is personal, never autoplayed or forcibly synchronized; the room still never requests camera or microphone permissions. Preserve the existing typography, palette, LED seating and reduced-motion behavior.
